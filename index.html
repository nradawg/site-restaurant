Our Story

Started on the Street. Stayed Real.

Fuego started as a weekend pop-up in East LA. Two friends, a grill, and a dream to make the best street tacos in the city. Five years later, we're on Sunset Blvd — but the recipes haven't changed. Every tortilla is pressed fresh. Every salsa is made daily.

No shortcuts. No freezers. Just real food made with love.
